Ask Bing
Which iOS version do I need?

Clipboard requires iOS 27 or later.

Does it need setup?

No manual setup is required. Add the shortcut and start using it.

What can I save?

The shortcut is designed for text, links, and images.

Does it silently save everything I copy?

The save workflow is triggered by running the shortcut. The product does not promise a silent record of every copy across your device.

Where are my saved items kept?

The shortcut stores your saved collection on your own device. No extra third-party app is required.
