05A few answers

A question
before you start?

A few things to know about Daily Journal. If something is still unclear, send me a note.

Ask Bing
Are both shortcuts free?

Yes. Daily Journal and its Control Centre companion are both free to add.

What setup is needed?

Create seven separate lists in Apple Reminders, named exactly Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. Use the capital first letter and spelling shown.

What does the Control Centre companion do?

It adds a memo to today’s journal, adds a memo to a selected note, creates a new quick note, or syncs new to-dos into today’s journal.

Do new to-dos appear automatically?

Use the Control Centre companion’s sync action to refresh the current day’s journal with new to-dos. This is an action you trigger.

Do I need another app or account?

The workflow uses Apple Shortcuts and built-in Apple apps, including Notes and Reminders. No extra third-party app is required.
