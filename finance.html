05A few answers

A question
before you start?

A few things to know about Finance Tracker. If something is still unclear, send me a note.

Ask Bing
What do I receive?

A Notion Finance Tracker, its Apple Shortcuts workflow, and a setup guide included within the product. Purchase and delivery are handled through Payhip.

Does it need setup?

Yes. Follow the included guide to set up your Notion workspace and connect the shortcut before logging transactions.

Can I export my data?

Yes. There are dedicated CSV export views for Transactions and Months. Open the relevant export view in Notion and use Notion’s export option. The export is a manual action.

Where does my finance information live?

Your transaction data is stored in your own Notion workspace. The shortcut is the quick-entry part of the workflow.

Can I use it on Android?

The shortcut workflow is made for Apple Shortcuts. The Notion workspace can be opened wherever you use Notion, but the shortcut itself is for Apple devices.
